# Kid story

Happy path for a kid joining OpenSouthKids.

1. The kid opens the web app.
2. The kid clicks **I want to register**.
3. The kid enters their data locally. When they submit, the app generates a temporary registration QR.
4. At the desk, staff scan the registration QR and register the kid.
5. The desk shows or prints an authorized kid QR that becomes the kid login credential.
6. The kid clicks **Access** and scans the authorized kid QR to log in.
7. If the kid is at home, they can log in with the same authorized QR if they have it printed or saved on another device.
8. The kid sees an empty passport and can open a map of activities.
9. The kid goes to activity 3, scans the activity QR, and joins its wait list.
10. The kid can only be on one wait list at a time.
11. The kid completes activity 3.
12. The activity lead scans the kid QR and marks activity 3 as completed.
13. The kid sees activity 3 completed in their passport.
14. The kid clicks **Friends**.
15. The kid can show their QR, or scan another kid QR, to become a follower.
16. The kid completes 4 activities.
17. The top bar shows a **1 wheel shot** button.
